How Apostille facilitation works

From your document to an Apostille

Send a clear scan, the place the document was issued, and the country where it will be used. We tell you the path, the price, and the current processing time before we start.

1

Tell us the country

Hague Convention countries take an Apostille from the state that issued the document, or a U.S. Department of State Apostille for a federal document. Other countries need authentication and embassy or consulate legalization. The destination country and the place the document was issued decide the process.

2

We prepare the document

Vital records need a certified copy. Many private documents need a California notary acknowledgment first. We come to your home, office, hospital, or care facility in Tracy and nearby cities for that signing.

3

We submit and follow up

The California Secretary of State issues the Apostille for California documents. A document issued in another state is Apostilled by that state through our network of Apostille facilitators. We submit the packet and return the Apostilled original to you.

Apostille or authentication

The right document authentication for the country

California Apostille

The California Secretary of State Apostilles California public documents and documents notarized by a California notary public. That covers most birth certificates, marriage certificates, diplomas, and powers of attorney issued or signed here. Seth Notary Services LLC is a private Apostille facilitator. We are not the Secretary of State, and we do not issue the Apostille ourselves.

Embassy and consulate legalization - Authentication

Countries that are not in the Hague Apostille Convention do not accept an Apostille. Those documents follow an authentication chain and then embassy or consulate legalization.

Can you Apostille a document issued in another state?

Yes. An out-of-state Apostille is issued by the Secretary of State, or the equivalent office, in the state that issued the document. A California Apostille cannot be placed on a birth certificate, marriage certificate, death certificate, divorce decree, diploma, transcript, or court record from another state. We coordinate that request with Apostille facilitators in the issuing state, including Texas, Florida, New York, Nevada, Arizona, Illinois, Washington, Georgia, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Ohio, North Carolina, Virginia, Colorado, Massachusetts, Michigan, Oregon, Maryland, Tennessee, and Hawaii. Email the scan and the state that issued the document.

Can you help with an Apostille for study abroad, marriage abroad, or immigration?

Yes. School transcripts and diplomas are the usual study-abroad documents. A birth certificate, single-status affidavit, or divorce decree is common for a marriage abroad. Immigration, OCI, and passport packets often include a birth certificate, marriage certificate, and a police or FBI clearance. We match each paper to a California Apostille or a U.S. Department of State Apostille.

What is the difference between a notary and an Apostille?

A notary public confirms a signature in California. An Apostille is the certificate a government office attaches so another country will accept that public document. The notary seal on the page is not the Apostille.

Can you Apostille a birth certificate, marriage certificate, or diploma?

Yes, when the paper is the right kind of copy. Birth, death, and marriage certificates must be certified copies. Diplomas and transcripts depend on the school and the country. We look at both before anything is notarized or mailed.

What are the documents typically Apostilled?

For India: birth, death, and marriage certificates, diplomas, powers of attorney, single-status affidavits, adoption papers, and OCI or passport support documents. For the Philippines and Mexico: birth and marriage certificates, divorce decrees, school records, powers of attorney, and travel or single-status affidavits. For Vietnam, since September 11, 2026: birth and marriage certificates, diplomas, powers of attorney, and police or FBI clearances. An FBI check is a federal Apostille. Commercial papers for Vietnam may still need legalization.

What should I send for a document review?

A readable scan, the country where the document will be presented, and the office that issued it. Do not sign a document that must be signed in front of the notary until the appointment. Bring a current passport, driver license, or other government photo ID to the signing.
