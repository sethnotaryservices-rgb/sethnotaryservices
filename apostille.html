Apostille or authentication

The right document authentication for the country

California apostille

The California Secretary of State apostilles California public documents and documents notarized by a California notary public. That covers most birth certificates, marriage certificates, diplomas, and powers of attorney issued or signed here. Seth Notary Services LLC is a private apostille facilitator. We are not the Secretary of State, and we do not issue the apostille ourselves.

Federal apostille and U.S. Department of State authentication

An FBI background check, an IRS Form 6166, and other federal records are authenticated in Washington, D.C., not in Sacramento. Sending an FBI Identity History Summary to the California Secretary of State will not produce an apostille. We sort that out before you pay a state fee.

Embassy and consulate legalization

Countries that are not in the Hague Apostille Convention do not accept an apostille. Those documents follow an authentication chain and then embassy or consulate legalization. India, the Philippines, and Mexico have long accepted an apostille. Vietnam has accepted an apostille for covered public documents since September 11, 2026. The United Kingdom, Canada, and most of Europe do as well. Tell us the country on the first call so the packet matches the office that will receive it.

What is the difference between a notary and an apostille?

A notary public confirms a signature in California. An apostille is the certificate a government office attaches so another country will accept that public document. The notary seal on the page is not the apostille.

Can you apostille a birth certificate, marriage certificate, or diploma?

Yes, when the paper is the right kind of copy. Birth, death, and marriage certificates must be certified copies. Diplomas and transcripts depend on the school and the country. We look at both before anything is notarized or mailed.

Which documents can be apostilled for India, the Philippines, Vietnam, or Mexico?

For India: birth, death, and marriage certificates, diplomas, powers of attorney, single-status affidavits, adoption papers, and OCI or passport support documents. For the Philippines and Mexico: birth and marriage certificates, divorce decrees, school records, powers of attorney, and travel or single-status affidavits. For Vietnam, since September 11, 2026: birth and marriage certificates, diplomas, powers of attorney, and police or FBI clearances. An FBI check is a federal apostille. Commercial papers for Vietnam may still need legalization.

Does an FBI background check get a California apostille?

An FBI background check is federal. It is authenticated by the U.S. Department of State. A California apostille is the wrong certificate for that document. We will say so before the request is sent.
