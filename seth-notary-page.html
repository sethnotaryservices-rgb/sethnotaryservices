What identification is required for notarization in California?

California law requires signers to present a current, unexpired government-issued photo ID (or an ID issued within the last 5 years). Accepted forms include a California driver’s license or ID card, U.S. passport or passport card, foreign passport stamped by USCIS, out-of-state driver's license, or U.S. military ID. All signers must be physically present and willing to sign.
