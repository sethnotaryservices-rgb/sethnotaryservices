How process serving works

From your papers to proof of service

Three steps. Call, text, or email the details, and we handle the service and the court-ready proof.

1

Tell us your case

Send the person or business to be served, the documents, where and when they can be found, the county and court, and your hearing or filing deadline. Every request is reviewed before service, and you get your quote.

2

We serve it

Personal service in San Joaquin, Alameda, Contra Costa, or Stanislaus County. You get a written update after every attempt with the date, time, and address. Routine service is up to 3 attempts within 10 days, and urgent service shortens that window.

3

You get court-ready proof

A court-ready Proof of Service and MC-030, signed by a registered process server and ready to file. We do not give legal advice or choose which papers you file.
